RE-BLOSSOM

Final Exhibition of the Students of Accademia d'Arte AD'A

Numeroventi · Florence, Italy

  • VenueNumeroventi, Via dei Pandolfini 20, Florence
  • InaugurationWednesday 2 June 2021, 3 PM to 7 PM
  • Work shownNot My Chosen Road, 2021, acrylic on canvas, 10x10cm. Other than the paintings, there are postcards made with etching, 15x10cm.

About the work

This artwork reflects my personal journey toward a dream I was never certain I could reach. It explores the restrictions on Palestinian movement and the discrimination I experience as a green ID holder. Every time I travel, I must pass through Jordan; I cannot use Al-Lod Airport or freely choose my route. The work also evokes Gaza International Airport, a road I will never be able to take, as it was destroyed in 2001.
