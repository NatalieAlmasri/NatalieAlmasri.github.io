The Artist

A Story
Told in Layers

My work is rooted in the land, the memory, and the weight of belonging to a place that lives in the imagination as much as in reality.

I'm Natalie Almasri — a Palestinian artist working across sculpture, mixed media, oil painting, and drawing. I grew up between cultures, and my art has always been the place where those tensions dissolve into something honest.

My sculptures speak of impermanence and resilience — crafted from clay, found objects, and materials that carry their own history. My paintings explore colour as emotion: the amber of olive groves, the grey-blue of Mediterranean mornings, the deep violet of quiet grief that becomes quiet strength.

I believe art doesn't need to explain itself. It only needs to make you feel less alone.
